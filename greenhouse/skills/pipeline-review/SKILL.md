In your first turn also read `page/PAGE.md` (it sets how the answer is delivered), alongside step 1's call. Budget: 8-11 calls on the default tools, 15-18 with every enhancement; paging adds at most one call per list. No Greenhouse writes.

## Steps

1. **Resolve the req** (1 call; 2 with the status "all" re-search). Take the job name or id, and the hiring manager's name when given ("sync with Dana"). Set `NOW` (UTC). Name: `search_jobs(search: <name>, status: "open", per_page: 10, fields: ["id", "name", "status", "requisition_id", "opened_at", "closed_at", "confidential", "is_template"])`. One row → `JOB`. Several → show name, req id and opened date, ask which; never guess. None → re-issue once with `status: "all"`; a closed or draft match is reviewable, say so. Still none → "No job I can see matches <name>; private jobs and other teams' jobs may be hidden." and stop. Given an id: `get_job(job_id: <id>)`; `[]` → "not found or not visible to you", stop. `is_template` true: no pipeline, stop.

2. **Plan, applications and identity** (one parallel batch, 4-5 calls plus the table rows needing only `JOB`).
   - `list_job_stages(job_id: JOB, active: true, per_page: 100, fields: ["id", "name", "sort_order"])` → `STAGES` by `sort_order`, n = count. `REVIEW` = the stage named Application Review, else the first; `FIRST_INTERVIEW` = the first later stage with Screen, Interview, Onsite or Panel in its name and without Pre-Screen, Assessment or Test, else the next; `LATE` = the last three; `FINAL` = the last stage with neither Offer nor Reference in its name.
   - `list_applications(job_id: JOB, status: "active", prospect: false, per_page: 500, fields: ["id", "candidate_id", "job_interview_stage_id", "stage_name", "last_activity_at", "needs_decision", "created_at", "recruiter_id", "coordinator_id", "source_id"])` → `ACTIVE`.
   - `list_applications(job_id: JOB, status: "rejected", prospect: false, per_page: 500, fields: ["id", "candidate_id", "job_interview_stage_id", "stage_name", "created_at", "rejected_at", "rejection_reason_id"])` → `ALL_REJECTED`, in one read; rows whose `rejected_at` is within 30 days = `REJECTED`. Follow its cursor once (1,000 rows); more: `REJECTED` and the derived column cover the rows read, and say "rejections: the first 1,000 read".
   - `list_applications(job_id: JOB, status: "hired", prospect: false, per_page: 500, fields: ["id"])` → `HIRED`.
   - Identity, optional: `ME` = the user id from a "Greenhouse profile: ... user_id=" line the user has given you (in this conversation or in the instructions they wrote for you); else `whoami` when it is listed, in this batch; else, when they have given you their login email the same way, `list_users(primary_email: <email>, fields: ["id", "first_name"])`. Never ask for the email; without `ME` the draft guard covers every row with a recent draft and the message is signed only with a first name you already know.
   - Paging: one `cursor` follow per other list; still full after it: "at least" on its total and the scope line.

3. **Names, feedback and reasons** (two steps in the same turn).
   - 3a, one parallel batch (2-3 calls, plus table rows needing these ids); `ACTIVE` empty: skip its two id-filtered reads. `ROSTER` = `ACTIVE` rows in `LATE`; under three rows, walk back one stage at a time until three or the plan runs out, never cutting inside a stage; always add rows past `REVIEW` with `needs_decision` true. Order: latest stage first, then oldest `last_activity_at`; cap at 50 and count the rest. `SCORED` = `ROSTER` ids plus other `ACTIVE` ids past `REVIEW`, nearest stage first, up to 50.
     - `list_scorecards(application_ids: SCORED, per_page: 500, fields: ["id", "application_id", "interviewer_id", "status", "candidate_rating", "interviewed_at", "submitted_at"])`. Never request notes fields. A non-roster row with a draft joins `ROSTER`.
     - `list_candidates(ids: <SCORED candidate ids>, per_page: 50, fields: ["id", "first_name", "last_name", "preferred_name", "private"])`.
     - `list_rejection_reasons(include_defaults: true, per_page: 500, fields: ["id", "name", "type"])` only when `REJECTED` is not empty.
   - 3b, after the scorecards return (1 call): `list_users(ids: <the most common recruiter_id and coordinator_id on ACTIVE, hiring-manager user_ids, interviewer_id of every draft, ME when its first name is unknown; at most 50>, per_page: 50, fields: ["id", "name", "first_name", "last_name"])`; skip it when the id set is empty.

4. **Compute (no calls).** Quiet = no activity recorded for 7 or more days (the one quiet number here).
   - **Funnel** row per stage: active now; rejected from here in 30 days (`REJECTED` by `job_interview_stage_id`); "Reached at least (derived)" over `ACTIVE`, `ALL_REJECTED` and `HIRED`: reached stage k = last known stage is k or later (active: current stage; rejected: the stage rejected from; hired: every stage). Stages outside the plan share one "Retired stage" row. Pass-through = reached k+1 over reached k among resolved (rejected and hired) applications: a percentage when 20 or more resolved reached k, "early signal" with counts at 10-19, counts alone under 10, a dash when nobody is resolved; "median" only from 3 or more values, rounded up to whole days.
   - **Bottleneck**: the stage with the most active candidates; tie → the oldest median `last_activity_at` (median `days_in_stage` with stage history). Furthest = the latest stage holding an active row, "stage k of n".
   - **Risks**, inclusive, worst first, printed with the req's numbers and the why: *Nobody past the first interview* (open 30+ days, nobody active beyond `FIRST_INTERVIEW`: a month should get someone past a first round); *Slow for its age* (open 60+ days, nobody in `FINAL` or later: two months without a finalist points at the funnel or the bar); *Gone quiet* (3+ active past `REVIEW`, half or more quiet: a week untouched is when candidates accept other interviews); *Thin pipeline* (2 or fewer active past `REVIEW`: one withdrawal empties the late stages). Status word, first that holds: **At risk** (first rule) · **Slow** (second) · **Watch** (only Gone quiet or Thin pipeline) · **On track** (no rule fires and someone in `FINAL` or later is not quiet) · **Hired, still open** (a hire, nobody active; checked before the rules) · **In progress** (default).
   - **Roster** row per `ROSTER` application: name (`preferred_name`, else first and last), stage, days quiet or in stage, feedback "n complete, m started but not submitted" over every scorecard on it (none at all: "no scorecards that you can see"), a tally of `candidate_rating` over `status: complete` rows (Strong Yes / Yes / No / Definitely Not / No Decision), `needs_decision` flag, interview and source when visible. Draft guard, for drafts with `interviewed_at` within 30 days only: when `ME` holds one, or `ME` is unknown and the row has one, print "tally hidden while a scorecard is unsubmitted" instead of the tally; an older draft reads "stale draft from <date>" and hides nothing. A failed scorecard read prints "feedback: not checked", never "0 complete". `private` true: the row says "Private candidate: don't forward"; the message says "a private candidate", link only.
   - **Rejections**: group `REJECTED` by reason and `type.key` (ignore case): `we_rejected_them` → "we declined", `they_rejected_us` → "withdrew", else "other or none recorded"; a blank `rejection_reason_id` reads "No reason recorded", sorts first and is flagged.
   - **Questions for the sync**: the first four that apply, in order, one per candidate and at most two quiet rows so a slow or thin req keeps its question, with numbers: decision waiting ("<Name> has a decision waiting since <latest complete-scorecard `submitted_at`, else `last_activity_at`>: what's the call?"), a draft within 30 days ("<interviewer>'s scorecard on <Name> dated <date> is still a draft: can you chase it?"), quiet roster row ("<Name> has had no activity recorded for n days in <stage>: what's the next step?"), slow or thin ("<req> has been open n days and nobody is past <stage>: is it who's applying, or where we set the bar?" / "Only n past review: what's the sourcing plan this week?"). Manager's stages = `FIRST_INTERVIEW` through `FINAL`, minus names containing Recruiter, Preliminary, Phone Screen or Reference; their questions go under "Ask the hiring manager", the rest under "Stays with you" (`REVIEW` first as "n in review, longest d days"; offers in `Created` over 7 days join here when visible).
   - **Hiring-manager message**, under 120 words, rendered first and always, shaped as in Output. Sentences by the first rung that holds: hired and closed · someone in Offer, by name · someone in `FINAL`, by name · slow (days open, furthest stage) · furthest stage past `REVIEW` and how many stand there · "Quiet week on <req>: no activity recorded on the n in process". Name only candidates active in `FINAL` or Offer; everyone else a count; rejected candidates are never named; no assessment words; no ratings, scorecard text, contact details or compensation; the most common rejection reason, quoted, only when 3 or more rejections carry it. The addressee follows the `list_job_hiring_managers` row below; never ask before rendering. `confidential` true: add "Confidential job: share only with its hiring team."

5. **Render** per Output. Headline, first that holds: closed req → "n candidates are still active on a closed req: they probably never heard back"; one stage holds half or more of `ACTIVE` → "<stage> holds n of N active candidates", with "(p%)" only when N is 10 or more; a decision waiting past `REVIEW` → "n candidates are waiting on a decision on <req>"; else "N active on <req>, nobody waiting on a decision". With a chat or email tool, offer "Want this message sent to <HM name, or 'your hiring manager'>?"; on yes show the text and recipient with "Confirm <recipient> is on this job's hiring team; Greenhouse access isn't checked for them." and `Reply "yes" to send.`; send only on that yes: one message, to a person, never a channel.
